[setpos.gg](https://setpos.gg) has free CS2 instant smoke lineups from every spawn, made from pro demos.

- [cs2-minimaps](https://github.com/setposgg/cs2-minimaps): vector CS2 minimaps in dark and light, with PNGs and calibration. CC BY 4.0.
- [cs2-spawns](https://github.com/setposgg/cs2-spawns): every competitive spawn with `setpos` and `setpos; setang` commands. CC BY 4.0.
- [cs2-crosshair](https://github.com/setposgg/cs2-crosshair): crosshair share codes, exact drawing and the grenade reticle. MIT.
